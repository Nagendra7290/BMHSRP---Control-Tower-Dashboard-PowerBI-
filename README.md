## Project Overview

This project delivers a high-impact analytical dashboard designed to monitor and optimize operations within the BMHSRP (High Security Registration Plate) ecosystem.

The dashboard enables real-time tracking of vehicle registrations, OEM performance, dealer productivity, fuel trends, and affixation workflows. It supports data-driven decision-making across supply chain and control tower operations.

This solution is built for Operations Heads, Control Tower Teams, and Business Decision-Makers who require visibility into end-to-end operational performance.

---

## Business Objectives

- Track daily and monthly vehicle registration trends  
- Monitor OEM and dealer performance metrics  
- Identify operational bottlenecks and inefficiencies  
- Improve upload compliance and data accuracy  
- Enable data-driven supply chain optimization  

---

## Dashboard Highlights

- Total Vehicles Analyzed: 57,170+  
- Total Dealers: 519  
- Total OEMs: 33  
- Upload Compliance: 96 percent  
- Dominant Segment: Petrol and LMV vehicles  

---

## Key Insights

### Vehicle Trends

Vehicle registrations show a consistent spike toward the end of each month, indicating deadline-driven processing behavior. There is a noticeable slowdown during mid-month periods, suggesting operational inefficiencies or reduced demand cycles.

The LMV (Light Motor Vehicle) segment contributes the majority share of total registrations, making it the most critical category for operational focus.

---

### Dealer Performance

A small group of top-performing dealers contributes a significant portion of total volume. This indicates dependency on a limited number of high-performing entities.

There is a strong opportunity to optimize the long-tail dealer network by improving performance consistency and accountability across lower-performing dealers.

---

### OEM Analysis

The market is highly concentrated among a few key manufacturers, primarily Maruti, Hyundai, Hero, and Bajaj. This concentration introduces a potential risk due to dependency on limited OEMs.

Diversification strategies and deeper OEM-level performance tracking can help mitigate operational risks.

---

### Fuel Insights

Approximately 80 percent of registered vehicles are petrol-based, making it the dominant fuel category.

Electric vehicle adoption remains minimal, highlighting a significant opportunity for future growth, forecasting, and infrastructure planning.

---

## Data Model

The dashboard is built on a structured data model with the following key dimensions:

- OEM Name: Vehicle manufacturer  
- Dealer ID: Unique identifier for each dealer  
- Vehicle Class: Transport or Non-Transport classification  
- Fuel Type: Petrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric  
- Upload Status: Uploaded or Pending records  
- Date Fields: Order Date, Assignment Date, Registration Date
